# Feature: ZX Spectrum BMX Ramp Jump Game

## Summary

A side scrolling ZX Spectrum game where the user pedals a bike to jump
a ramp over a number of people laying down behind the ramp

## Game features

- on starting the game, the user on a bike starts on the left of the screen
- a ramp is displayed, low end to the left, high end to the right
- to the righthand side of the ramp are a number of people laying down
- the number of peoplay laying down starts at 1 and then increments
after each successful jump
- the player increases the speed of the bike by pedalling with the 
O and P keys
- based on the speed, the bike hits the ramp and lauches into the air
- on landing, if the player doesn't hit any of the people, the score
is increased by 1
- the player has 3 attempts
- 1 attempt is lost for each person hit
- after attempts are lost the game ends and score is displayed
